Rooster Rojo
Tequila

Made from the finest 100% pure Blue Weber agave, Rooster Rojo is aged in controlled humidity to ensure a stable and sustainable process. Each batch is blended at “Fabrica de Tequilas Finos” distillery, located in the city of Tequila, to ensure a consistent and delivered-to-perfection taste palate.

Fábrica de Tequilas Finos

Tequila, Jalisco

Rooster Rojo is made in the town of Tequila, in a colonial building from 1923. Distilling started there in 2000, right at the foot of the Tequila volcano.
